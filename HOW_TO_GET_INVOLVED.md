# How to Get Involved

This public repository is the contact point for the Compiled Intelligence research project.

The active working laboratory is private.

## If you are interested

Open a GitHub issue in this repository with the title:

```
Research Interest
```

In the issue, briefly describe:

1. what part of the research interests you;
2. whether you want to discuss, reproduce, critique, or contribute;
3. any relevant technical background you want me to know about;
4. what kind of involvement you are asking for.

You do **not** need an academic affiliation or formal credentials to contact me.

## Access

There is no automatic membership in the private research repository.

Public interest, discussion, criticism, or independent reproduction can happen entirely through this repository.

If closer collaboration or access to private working material makes sense, I will decide that individually after we communicate.

## Contributions

Do not send proprietary, confidential, restricted, or third-party material that you do not have the right to share.

If you want to contribute original research or code, describe it first so the scope and research-use terms are clear before anything is incorporated.

## What I am interested in hearing from

Relevant areas include:

- deterministic or analytical weight construction;
- alternatives to ordinary gradient training;
- mechanistic interpretability;
- attention and residual-state behavior;
- explicit symbolic or attribute representations;
- procedural memory;
- model-state intervention;
- replication and falsification;
- unusual architectures grounded in measurable mechanisms.

Technical disagreement is welcome when it is specific enough to test.
